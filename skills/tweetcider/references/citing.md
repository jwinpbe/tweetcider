# Citing tweets

Conventions for presenting posts in an answer. The core template is in
`SKILL.md`; this covers the rest.

- **Verbatim bodies.** Never paraphrase inside a blockquote. Trim with `[…]`,
  never silently. Commentary goes outside the quote.
- **Attribution under the quote.** Bold display name, handle linked to the post
  permalink, date. After the first mention, plain `@handle`.
- **Threads.** Numbered list in author order, one quote each. For long threads,
  summarize first, then quote only the load-bearing posts.
- **Quote tweets.** Nest the quoted post one level deeper with `>>`.
- **Several reactions.** Bulleted one-line quotes, or a `Who | Said` table to
  compare. Quote the relevant reactions and say how many you reviewed.
- **Media.** `*(photo)*` or `*(video, 0:42)*` plus the URL.
- **Tombstones.** `*(unavailable: deleted)*`.
- **Grouping.** `---` between groups (thread, replies, quotes). No emoji.
- **Counts.** Leave likes and reposts out unless the user asked about reach or
  you are ranking replies.
